# Evertophy — Core Ontology

## In Search of a Common Language Between the Disconnected Pieces of Science

Modern science is the most reliable structure of knowledge humanity has ever built. It rests on solid findings that have been tested by experiment, confirmed by observation and measured again and again. From collisions of subatomic particles to the rotation speeds of galaxies billions of light years away, from the faint tremor of gravitational waves to the ticking of the most precise atomic clocks, we can measure and calculate with extraordinary accuracy. General Relativity, Quantum Mechanics, the Standard Model and Cosmology are each monuments of success, working flawlessly within their own domains.

Yet when we place these four great structures side by side and try to read the same universe as a single whole, we find that some pieces do not quite fit together, and that these fields do not speak each other's language:

- We measure the gravitational effect of roughly 95% of the matter and energy in the universe, but we do not know what it is; "dark matter" and "dark energy" are, in a sense, names for our ignorance.
- Many of the fundamental equations of physics work the same way forwards and backwards in "time" (they are symmetric), while in the world we live in and measure, time flows in one direction.
- Quantum mechanics works with extraordinary accuracy, yet there is still no agreement on what a "measurement" actually is.
- General Relativity and quantum theory are flawless in their own domains, but they do not come together. At the centre of black holes the equations run into singularities of "infinite density and zero volume" that go beyond physical reality, and at the beginning of the universe into the debate over whether the question "What was there before the Big Bang?" is even meaningful.

These are not failures of science; they are its present limits. The measurement is there; the experiment is there. What is missing is the meaning that connects those measurements to one another.

**Evertophy** was born from exactly these gaps. Conducted since 20 July 2025 by independent researcher Uğur Yılmaz, it is not a project that tries to prove an ontology of its own invention, nor one that tries to fit science into a pre-designed model. On the contrary, it is a research programme that, remaining fully faithful to the fundamental methods of science, works **from results measured by experiment and observation** to enable the Standard Model, General Relativity, Quantum Mechanics and Cosmology to speak a common language.

Its aim is not to replace any proven model or theorem. It seeks to be a **"Rosetta Stone" — a common grammar of translation** — able to bring together existing scientific frameworks that speak different languages. In its own words, Evertophy is not a claim to an answer, but a disciplined search for one.

## The Basic Rule of Method: Measurement Is Unconditional, Interpretation Is Optional

Because Evertophy refuses to fit science to its own ontology, every line of this document is bound to a single basic rule:

> **"The outputs of Experiment, Measurement and Observation are accepted UNCONDITIONALLY; the layers of postulate and interpretation are treated as OPTIONAL and UNINTERPRETED."**

In scientific reality, "the measurement itself" and "the interpretation built on the measurement" are not the same thing. The track a particle leaves in a detector is a measurement; whether that track is a "collapse of the wave function", a "branching of worlds" or something else is an interpretation. That galaxies rotate faster than expected is an observation; whether this comes from an invisible particle or from the behaviour of gravitation at different scales is an interpretation.

Evertophy approaches these layers with three clear principles:

1. **Optional:** It treats none of the existing scientific interpretations as mandatory and rejects none of them; it keeps them all on the table as options.
2. **Uninterpreted:** It loads no interpretation onto the measurement itself. It takes the raw finding as it is and places its own reading on top of it as a separate layer. The reading Evertophy proposes is also an option; it is a candidate to be tested alongside the others, not to replace them. If a measurement contradicts one of Evertophy's readings, it is not the measurement that must change, but Evertophy's reading.
3. **Physical reality and mathematics (the third principle):** The mathematical flawlessness of a theory does not, by itself, count as physical reality (experiment, measurement, observation). By contrast, every mathematical representation that correctly reproduces a measurement is accepted unconditionally within the scope of that measurement. The same orbit can be written in the language of Newton or of General Relativity; neither representation is ontologically prior to the other. **MODEL ≠ ONTOLOGY:** the observational success of a model does not show that every mathematical tool it uses is a fundamental ontological entity.

## Ontological Architecture: Three Phases, Two Levels and the Working Mechanism of the Universe

Evertophy's basic proposal is this: reality does not consist of a single plane, or only of particles made of matter; like the ice, liquid and vapour states of water, it is realized in a **three-phase** structure. This time, however, the phases are not phases of matter but of **reality itself**. Physical laws are not rules that exist arbitrarily inside these phases; they are forms that arise from the relations and the structure of transitions between the phases.

**The heart of the framework (the basic working mechanism of the universe):** In Evertophy, the **tendency to cluster** on the side of matter and the **tendency to expand** on the side of the fabric / c⁺ are read together, in the language of inter-phase tension and transformation. These two tendencies produce a continuous relation and transformation between c₀, Cₘ and c⁺; this is the main narrative axis of the framework.

The holistic structure that the three phases form together is called the **Dynamic Universe (Uₓ)**. Within Uₓ, the three phases do not form a linear chain; they form a **complete bidirectional triangle (K3)** in which each is directly connected to the other two (c₀ ↔ Cₘ, Cₘ ↔ c⁺, c₀ ↔ c⁺).

### 1. Cₘ — the Phase of Matter, Measurement and Emergent Time (the Epistemic Centre)

The phase in which differentiated massive matter, atoms, particles and the processes of measurement exist — the phase we interact with directly.

- **The only home of time and the metric:** Physical time, three-dimensional flat Euclidean base space (E³, with vanishing curvature), the metric tensor (g_μν) and the light-speed limit are defined **only in Cₘ**. The four-dimensional curved spacetime of General Relativity is not a fundamental ontological entity; it is the **effective 3+1 readout geometry** on Cₘ's flat Euclidean base space.
- **Emergent time:** Physical time is not an independent external clock that flows on its own; it is a reading that arises from distinguishable change of state in the Cₘ phase (written dt = Γ 𝒢_Cₘ dτ).
- **The kinematic membership band and the speed of light:** The kinematic band required for a structure to be a member of the Cₘ phase as matter is 0 < v < c. The "0" at the lower end is not a local laboratory coordinate speed (v_lab = 0 does not imply c₀); it is a relative **membership reading** that includes the object's internal dynamics, spin structure and cosmological drift. Before it is a speed limit, the speed of light c is the **consistency constraint** that sets the information-processing and record resolution of the Cₘ phase (in Cₘ, c is a point range: [c, c]).
- **The liminal shared boundary (v = c):** A massive body cannot reach c by accelerating within Cₘ; at this limit the issue is not an acceleration but a phase transition into the c⁺ regime. Light itself (the photon) and gravitational waves propagate at v = c, on the **liminal shared boundary** of the Cₘ and c⁺ phases (lying on the boundary ≠ phase membership; a vanishing proper time of the photon does not make the photon a member of c⁺).
- **A physical redefinition of the observer principle:** The observer is not "consciousness" or a privileged metaphysical class; the observer is **the matter phase Cₘ itself** — that is, the role of every system in Cₘ that produces physical records and readouts.

### 2. c₀ — the Pre-temporal Potential / Black-Hole Phase

The phase in which physically measurable matter loses its atomic and particle properties, and all particles merge, to a degree converging to the metric-gapless state at the Planck scale, into an **integrated single structure**.

- **Black hole ≡ c₀ (author's ruling):** In the Evertophy ontology, a black hole and the c₀ phase are ontologically identical. The singularity of "infinite density and zero volume" that classical General Relativity gives for the centre of a black hole is read as a **regime error** that goes beyond the theory's domain of validity.
- **The metric-gapless single structure and its capacity:** The structure inside the event horizon is neither an annihilation nor a "tiny Planck-sized point"; it is a contiguous, finite mass structure in a single form, in which almost no metric gap remains between the particles. The counting unit of the core capacity is the Planck mass (N_c₀ = M/m_P), and its density cannot exceed the Planck density (ρ_c₀ ≤ ρ_P ≈ 5.16 × 10⁹⁶ kg/m³). A more massive black hole is **not denser**; it has a **larger volume** of the same metric-gapless structure (R_c₀ ∝ M^(1/3) in the constant-density realization) and a larger horizon (R_s ∝ M, A ∝ M²). For a black hole of one solar mass, the lower-bound radius of this single structure is about 4.5 × 10⁻²³ m — far larger than the Planck length and far smaller than the horizon of about 3 km. The event horizon and the boundary of the single structure are therefore not identical, and the quantities V, R and ρ of c₀ are not the internal geometry of c₀ but effective readout parameters taken from Cₘ.
- **Time has not stopped; it has not yet been established (⊥₀):** No notion of a clock can be set up in c₀ (T(c₀) = ⊥₀ ≠ 0). Time there does not "flow slowly" and is not "a frozen river"; it has not yet been born.
- **c₀ ≠ absolute zero, and the BEC analogy:** Absolute zero (0 kelvin) is the thermodynamic ground state within the Cₘ phase itself; quantum zero-point motion continues there and atomic clocks keep running. In c₀, by contrast, temperature and heat are undefined. A Bose–Einstein condensate (BEC) is not c₀ itself, but **the environment in the matter phase closest to the c₀ environment** (an analogy of collective single structure). The Hawking temperature attributed to a black hole is not the internal temperature of c₀, but a candidate reading: the thermal readout the horizon gives to a distant Cₘ observer.

### 3. c⁺ — the Atemporal Upper Phase / Archive / Dark Sector and Vacuum Phase

The upper layer of coordination and archive in which massive matter support (the support of matter in Cₘ goes to the empty set), Euclidean geometry (the Cₘ metric becomes ⊥₊) and the time established in Cₘ are **dissolved (⊥₊)**.

- **The canonical identity (c⁺ ≡ dark matter ≡ dark energy ≡ vacuum):** Everything in the universe that is not in the form of matter or a black hole is the c⁺ phase. Every region read as "empty space" on the Cₘ screen — from the voids between galaxies down to **the space between the particles that make up the atom** — is the Cₘ reading of c⁺. Dark matter and dark energy, which astrophysicists search for as two separate mysterious particles or fluids, are not two separate entities; they are context-dependent readings of the same c⁺ phase on the Cₘ screen:
  - *At galaxy and cluster scale (clustered c⁺):* clustering together with baryonic matter under the coherence of the Force of Attraction, it holds galactic rotation curves (RAR) together and produces lensing — the **dark-matter reading**. In the words of the document's "glasses analogy": *"They are looking for the invisible object that distorts the image; we are saying that the refractive index of the medium varies."*
  - *In cosmic voids (expansion-sustaining c⁺):* when the density of matter falls, the sealing constraint relaxes and c⁺ returns to its own natural relaxation regime (a volumetric phase expansion); the Cₘ observer measures this as the accelerating expansion of the universe — the **dark-energy reading**.
  - *At laboratory scale and inside the atom:* it gives the **vacuum reading**. The vacuum state of quantum field theory and measured vacuum phenomena such as the Casimir effect are the computational language of this ontological identity within Cₘ.
- **The beyond-c regime:** c⁺ is the beyond-c upper regime in which the 0 < v < c band is dissolved; but since neither time nor metric distance is defined there, this does not mean a Cₘ-type "faster-than-light particle motion" (dx/dt > c).

### 4. The Global Level: U₀, Uₓ and U₊

While the phase level forms a triangle inside, two limiting conditions of the whole Dynamic Universe (Uₓ) stand at the **global level of the universe**: the **Absolute Core (U₀)** and **Absolute Dissolution ≡ Absolute Rupture (U₊)**. None of these is a phase, and none can be the end point of an ordinary phase gate (U₀, Uₓ, U₊ ∉ {c₀, Cₘ, c⁺}).

- **U₀ and the Big Bang (joint birth):** U₀ is the generative pre-layer in which all structures exist potentially in the form of a single, metric-gapless structure at the Planck scale. When the whole of Uₓ collapses and crosses the critical saturation threshold at the boundary of U₀, the **Big Bang** takes place, and the three phases (c₀, Cₘ, c⁺) are born not one after another but **together, in the same constitution (co-genesis)**. Time is not an external clock flowing above the Big Bang; it is born together with Cₘ.
- **U₊ and the Big Rupture:** When the whole of Uₓ thins out and crosses the critical resolution threshold at the boundary of U₊, the **Big Rupture** takes place. The *Big Rip* of standard cosmology is an event that only ends the universe; in Evertophy, the Big Rupture is both an end and **the birth of a new Uₓ**.
- **Flexibility of the cycle and the Principle of Silence:** The global cycle is not a mandatory periodic clock or conveyor; the Dynamic Universe may or may not come to an end. There may be a direct relation between U₀ and U₊, but it has no representation in any known sense; **where there is no representation, Evertophy makes no ruling (the Principle of Silence)**.

## Basic Principles and Conceptual Locks: the Force of Attraction, X(x), the Seal and the Quantum Boundary

The following are critical ontological mechanisms that form the backbone of the Core Ontology (Sections 1–18) and are easily passed over in short summaries.

### 1. Postulate 0 — the Force of Attraction (Force of Attraction ≠ gravitation)

Evertophy's unnumbered and most fundamental postulate is the **Force of Attraction**. The conceptual lock is strict: in no context is the Force of Attraction "gravitation". At the ontological level, the Force of Attraction is a **phase-independent constraint coherence** that needs no metric and keeps the three phases consistent with one another in a balance. The four known fundamental interactions — gravitation, the strong nuclear force, the weak nuclear force and electromagnetism — are proposed as the different projections (Π by scale, mass and phase) that the Force of Attraction takes **according to scale, amount of mass and phase**. Gravitation is only its metric projection in the Cₘ phase. The document strictly forbids confusing five separate objects: Force of Attraction (ontology) ≠ gravitation (Cₘ) ≠ X ≠ g_μν ≠ G_eff.

### 2. The Dynamic Meta-Resolver X(x) and the Phase-Parameter Matrix

The single upper object that resolves which phase regime holds under given conditions, and which states are admissible, is the **dynamic meta-resolver X(x)**. X(x) is not a single scalar number, not a logistic function, not a particle and not the carrier of a new force; it is a staged meta-resolver that is sensitive to phase and context, has variable arity (it can take different sets of arguments in different phases), and gives a deterministic output for the same fully specified state (S₁ = S₂ ⇒ X(S₁) = X(S₂)). Its scalar readings are taken through projections ξₖ = πₖ(X), and a calculation made in the readout layer cannot, on its own, produce a fundamental number. So that the framework is not narrowed by an early choice of function, the full mathematical realization of X(x) has been **deliberately left open**.

- **The Phase-Parameter Matrix and five different "empties":** The Phase-Parameter Matrix, one of the core structures of the framework (PropertyFamily × Phase → Range or ⊥), sets out the status of every physical quantity in the three phases; X is not a row of this matrix but its meta-resolver. The matrix distinguishes five different notions of "empty / absent" from one another and from epistemic "missing data": ⊥₀ (not yet established in c₀) ≠ ⊥₊ (dissolved in c⁺) ≠ 0 (a true numerical zero) ≠ [0, 0] (an exact zero-width range) ≠ None (a stampless transition).
- **Map position ≠ phase membership, and the interior region:** An object approaching a corner of the phase map does not mean that it changes phase. Planets, atoms, electromagnetic and biological structures remain members of Cₘ while carrying continuous relations and constraints with all three phases; their natural working domain is the **interior region (𝒟_inner)**, where X resolves the constraints of the three phases together. In the nuclear domain, too, Evertophy modulates the **envelope (first order)** of the binding-energy curve; it does not invent magic numbers or shell closures (second order) and does not touch the shell model.

### 3. Gate ≠ Transport ≠ Readout, the Stamp Algebra and the Arrow of Time

In transitions between the three phases, three concepts are kept strictly apart. A **gate** is the structural permission of transition between two phases; **transport permission** requires a quantity to be intrinsically defined in the phases at both ends of the gate; a **readout** is the measurement in Cₘ of an effect sourced in an outer phase. Because time, three-dimensional space, the metric, intrinsic speed, the c limit and rest mass are defined only in Cₘ, they are **gate-locked (`GateLocked`) and cannot be carried through any gate into the outer phases**. This lock, however, does not prevent effects sourced in c₀ or c⁺ from being **read** in the language of Cₘ (source phase ≠ observation phase).

- **Sealing and the stamp monoid (M):** The phase gates are bidirectional on all three edges, but **the record channel (Cₘ → c⁺) is unidirectional**. The edges Δ₀ₘ and Δ₊ₘ carry stamps; the direct edge Δ₀₊, which does not touch Cₘ, is stampless (it emits no stamp, and its algebraic image is the identity e) and provides only X-coordination. Once a record is sealed into the c⁺ archive it cannot be undone; since there is no primitive operation of erasure (erase, pop, undo), the algebra of the archive is not a group but a **monoid**, in which the lock stamps are idempotent (κ₀ₘ² = κ₀ₘ, κ₊ₘ² = κ₊ₘ). The fully faithful, append-only **raw archive** is kept separate from the coarse **stamp projection**; time does not flow in the archive, and records are ordered only by **provenance order (A ⪯ A′)**.
- **Entropy and the conservation of energy at the level of Uₓ:** Physical time, thermodynamic entropy and archive order are not definitions of one another (physical time ≠ thermodynamic entropy ≠ archive order). By author's ruling, entropy is the dissolved state of Cₘ structure into c⁺; but **energy does not vanish**: both the record and the share of energy of the dissolving structure are sealed in c⁺, and total energy is conserved at the level of Uₓ through a global accounting principle (the Uₓ energy account includes both the energy measured in Cₘ and the energy sealed in c⁺).

### 4. The Quantum Boundary Regime and Compatible Readings

In Evertophy, the subatomic quantum level lies, like light, on the **phase regime–threshold boundary** between Cₘ and c⁺. (Massive particles are members of Cₘ on the kinematic axis, with β = v/c between 0 and 1, while showing boundary behaviour on a separate axis of phase coherence / quantum readout.) Quantum field theory is not an alternative to Evertophy but **the effective computational limit within the Cₘ phase**. Accepting all measurements of standard quantum mechanics unconditionally, the framework offers the following compatible and candidate readings:

- **Quantum entanglement and teleportation:** Two particles that appear far apart in Cₘ are connected through a single atemporal relation in the c⁺ layer. c⁺ is a **write-protected store of correlation**, but it is **not a communication or signal channel**; information transfer always requires Cₘ's classical communication channel (at most c), as in the 2025 Stuttgart quantum-teleportation experiment.
- **The Hartman effect (tunnelling):** The saturation of the tunnelling time at a constant value, rather than growing with barrier thickness, is read as the temporary loosening of the record in front of the barrier (unsealing), the use of a metricless and atemporal c⁺ phase shortcut, and resealing behind the barrier.
- **The double slit, delayed choice, neutrinos and production from the vacuum:** As long as a particle is not measured, it shows the wave-like behaviour of the c⁺–Cₘ boundary; at the moment of measurement it is sealed on the Cₘ screen as a particle (superposition = an unconfirmed draft area; measurement = commit). Neutrinos are read as the structures most weakly bound to the Cₘ phase, closest to the boundary between mass and light. Particle production from the vacuum in a strong electric field (the Schwinger mechanism) is, under the identity c⁺ ≡ vacuum, a local phase transition triggered by external energy on the Δ₊ₘ (c⁺ → Cₘ) edge.

### 5. The Range Formalism

To carry measurement uncertainties and model parameters honestly, physical quantities are written as interval-valued rather than as point values: P ∈ [P⁻, P⁺]. A degenerate zero-width range [P, P] is a fully valid, static and deterministic constant; interval-valued writing therefore does not claim that "every constant changes with time". This formalism does not by itself produce physical law. It separates the acceptance range (ΔX_acc), the derived prediction range (ΔX_pred) and the observation range (ΔX_obs) — if the prediction and observation ranges do not intersect, the claim is falsified — and it forbids vacuous ranges that fit any result (such as [0, ∞)), thereby establishing a discipline of falsification.

## Scientific Status: Why "0/10", and What Are the Open Debts?

The most concrete sign of Evertophy's scientific seriousness is that, instead of polishing its successes, it puts what it cannot yet do in the most visible place.

The framework's canonical numerical-prediction counter stands today at **0/10**. This means that for a result in Evertophy to count as "1/10", it must pass six strict conditions together: *(i) it must be derived directly from the ontology; (ii) its parameters and bridge assumptions must be fixed in advance; (iii) it must be distinguishable from the standard approach; (iv) it must be preregistered before the target data are seen; (v) its data lineage must be sealed; and (vi) it must be tested independently on blind data.* Today no item satisfies all six conditions together. Moreover, the multi-domain correlation line (Xa–Xz–Xt), once regarded as the framework's strongest empirical claim, was closed permanently not by an external referee's criticism but by the programme's own internal audit protocol, which identified three root errors in its analysis pipeline: a time series ordered by value rather than by time, index matching without a physical common key, and a mis-targeted permutation null test.
